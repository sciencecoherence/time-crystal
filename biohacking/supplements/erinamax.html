Substrate distinction

The real differentiator is not mushroom mythology, but active architecture density.

The source material makes a sharp distinction between fruiting-body marketing and mycelial relevance. Fruiting bodies may contain hericenones, but the mycelium is where the erinacines live, and those are the compounds most associated with meaningful blood-brain-barrier penetration and NGF-relevant action.

That is why liquid culture matters. Standard mycelium products are often grown on rice or oats and harvested with the grain still attached, which means a large part of the powder can become inert starch mass. In coherence language, the signal is buried in substrate noise.

ErinaMAX is more compelling because it uses pure liquid fermentation: the mycelium grows in broth, is separated out, and then freeze-dried. The result is a cleaner fungal biomass profile and a page that belongs in the rebuild, re-pattern, and neural restoration tier of the protocol map.
